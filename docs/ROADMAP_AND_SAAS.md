## Quality gates (V2 retained)
G1 Requirements: no unresolved critical scope/authority. G2 Architecture: reviewed integration capabilities, trust boundaries and spend ledger. G3 Implementation readiness: feature spec, dependencies, safe local config and permissions. G4 Code quality: build/lint/type/review, defects resolved. G5 Testing: acceptance, race, retry/unknown reconciliation, budget and tenant tests executed. G6 Security: no critical auth, spend, token, SSRF or privacy defects. G7 Deployment: recovery, migrations, release, operational kill switches verified. G8 Production verification: authorized deployment, smoke and remote state reconciliation, monitoring. Status PASS/FAIL/BLOCKED/NOT APPLICABLE/NOT EXECUTED. Report evidence, not estimated completion.

## SaaS migration plan
Phase A now: org_id on all tenant data, scoped memberships/services/workers/cache/assets/retrieval/queues, tenant-aware indexes and audit, isolated OAuth credentials, configurable source URLs and brand profiles, quotas and metering. One active org, private signup disabled; synthetic second-tenant tests required. No shared cross-tenant retrieval. Phase B after pilot: invite/onboarding, domain verification, tenant data export/delete, robust isolation load tests, data processing/privacy agreements, provider app review and customer authorization. Phase C: billing/entitlement service, India/global payment provider evaluation, webhook dedupe, invoice/tax review, plans, credit reservation/reconciliation, failed payments/grace period, customer support and admin. Ads spend charged directly to customer ad account unless separately approved future billing model; avoid pooled spend. Phase D: agencies, multiple brands per org, client permissions, optional white label, usage analytics and scaling based on measured bottlenecks.
Illustrative entry plan ₹999–1,499 per workspace/month, not validated pricing; use real pilot economics, tax, acquisition/support/payment costs and contribution margins before commercial commitments. Separate premium media and X charges or quota. No unlimited generation. Do not promise savings or marketing ROI.
